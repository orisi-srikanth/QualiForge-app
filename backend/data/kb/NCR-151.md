# Nonconformance Report: NCR-151
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Missing component: Missing conductor core wire in multi-strand industrial cable
- **Date:** 2026-09-28
- **Part Number:** CAB-IND-HARN-04
- **Proxy Category:** cable
- **Machine:** AUTO-STRIP-02
- **Defect Classification:** missing_component
- **Defect Family:** component_integrity
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual camera detected only 3 insulated conductors entering breakout gland instead of required 4-conductor specification.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Upstream cable reel splice joint had broken conductor wire that went undetected during outer jacket extruding.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Integrated high-frequency spark tester and magnetic inductive wire counter on jacket extrusion line.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantined reel batch; segmented and rejected 15 meters of defective cable run.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
