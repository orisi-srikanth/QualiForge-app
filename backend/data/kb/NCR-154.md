# Nonconformance Report: NCR-154
- **Title:** Surface impact dent on cylindrical shaft body during automated gantry transfer
- **Defect Type:** dent
- **Defect Family:** geometric_deformation
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** GANTRY-TRANSFER-01
- **Date:** 2026-10-01
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative

## Description
Visual inspection identified localized impact crater and depression dent on outer diameter of AISI 1045 precision shaft. Depth measured at 0.18 mm exceeding visual cosmetic acceptance limit.

## Confirmed Root Cause
Robotic gantry pick-and-place gripper deceleration curve profile was misconfigured in servo drive, causing part to strike intermediate transfer V-block during high-speed cycle.

## Resolution & Corrective Action
Updated servo motion deceleration profile with S-curve smoothing, installed urethane buffer padding on V-block staging nest, and added optical seating sensor.

## Containment Action
Quarantine 120 shafts from Batch BATCH-0245; 100% dial indicator inspection.
