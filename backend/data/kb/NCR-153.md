# Nonconformance Report: NCR-153
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Decoy: Wire insulation scoring from sharp grommet edge during bundle routing
- **Date:** 2026-10-05
- **Part Number:** CAB-IND-HARN-04
- **Proxy Category:** cable
- **Machine:** ROUTING-BENCH-04
- **Defect Classification:** insulation_or_wire_damage
- **Defect Family:** insulation_damage
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Surface scoring along outer cable jacket resembling automated stripper blade nicking.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Decoy investigation: Stripping machines verified calibrated. Root cause determined to be metal chassis passthrough grommet missing its nylon split-sleeve collar, scraping harness during manual bundle pull.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Installed captive snap-in protective nylon edge grommets on all assembly jigs and updated harness routing SOP.
- **Containment Action:** Inspected 50 completed harnesses; replaced damaged protective jackets.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
