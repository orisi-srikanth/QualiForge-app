# Nonconformance Report: NCR-031
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Chatter marks and surface roughness out of specification
- **Date:** 2026-05-19
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-04
- **Defect Classification:** Surface chatter / finish waviness
- **Incident Description:** High-frequency circumferential ripples observed on shaft surface. Vibration sensor logged 4.2 mm/s radial vibration.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Loose clamping collet sleeve and worn tailstock live center bearing causing harmonic resonance.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced tailstock live center and adjusted hydraulic chuck pressure to 28 bar.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
