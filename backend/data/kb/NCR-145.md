# Nonconformance Report: NCR-145
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Incorrect assembly: Pin terminal wire swap on multi-conductor cable harness
- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Part Number:** CAB-IND-HARN-04
- **Proxy Category:** cable
- **Machine:** HARNESS-JIG-03
- **Defect Classification:** incorrect_assembly
- **Defect Family:** assembly_error
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection identified swapped colored wire conductors (blue pin 3 and green pin 4 reversed) in 6-pin connector housing.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Wire harness assembly board routing guide peg for pin 3 was bent, leading assembly operator to misroute conductor into adjacent terminal guide slot.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced aluminum guide pegs with hardened steel keyed pins and implemented optical color-sequencing camera verification prior to connector latching.
- **Containment Action:** 100% electrical continuity and pinout test on all 65 harnesses in active production lot.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
