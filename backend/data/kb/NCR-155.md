# Nonconformance Report: NCR-155
- **Title:** Transverse hairline thermal crack on shaft step shoulder fillet from excessive plunge grinding infeed
- **Defect Type:** crack
- **Defect Family:** metallurgical_fracture
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** GRIND-01
- **Date:** 2026-10-03
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative

## Description
Automated vision inspection flagged transverse hairline crack propagating across bearing transition fillet of precision ground shaft. Dye penetrant check confirmed stress micro-crack.

## Confirmed Root Cause
Cylindrical plunge grinder infeed rate was set to 0.045 mm/sec instead of specified 0.020 mm/sec, combined with glazed CBN wheel surface causing localized thermal temper burn and tensile residual stress cracking.

## Resolution & Corrective Action
Dressed and balanced CBN grinding wheel, reduced rough infeed rate to 0.018 mm/sec, and increased high-pressure coolant flow directly onto grinding zone.

## Containment Action
100% eddy-current crack detection screening on all 85 pieces ground during shift.
