# Nonconformance Report: NCR-149
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Decoy: Thread damage caused by undersized blank chamfer angle rather than die wear
- **Date:** 2026-09-19
- **Part Number:** SCR-HEX-M8X40
- **Proxy Category:** screw
- **Machine:** THREAD-ROLLER-02
- **Defect Classification:** thread_damage
- **Defect Family:** machining_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Torn lead threads on screw tips following thread rolling. Die condition was suspected initially.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Decoy investigation: Thread rolling dies were new (< 5,000 cycles). Root cause traced upstream to CNC cold header blank cutting tool, which produced a 15-degree lead chamfer instead of the 45-degree specified chamfer.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Re-ground header chamfer tooling insert to 45 degrees +/- 1 degree and added optical shadowgraph inspection at blanking station.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantined 600 header blanks prior to rolling; sorted rolled parts.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
