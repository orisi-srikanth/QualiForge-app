# Nonconformance Report: NCR-142
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Dent deformation on metal nut rim from vibratory bowl feeder drop height
- **Date:** 2026-08-18
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** FEEDER-BOWL-01
- **Defect Classification:** dent_deformation
- **Defect Family:** geometric_deformation
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection identified localized edge dents and crater indentations on hexagonal flange corners. Average dent depth 0.25 mm exceeding visual aesthetic criteria.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Drop height from primary bulk hopper vibratory track to orientation spiral bowl was 450 mm without dampening baffle, causing metal-to-metal impact denting on case-hardened blanks.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Installed stepped urethane cascade baffles to limit free-fall distance to < 80 mm and adjusted vibratory hopper feed rate sensor.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantine 320 raw blanks; sort by optical profile projector.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
