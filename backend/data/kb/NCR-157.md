# Nonconformance Report: NCR-157
- **Title:** Misplaced retaining collar assembled 55mm into shaft main body instead of shoulder datum
- **Defect Type:** incorrect_assembly
- **Defect Family:** assembly_error
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** ASSY-PRESS-01
- **Date:** 2026-10-08
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative

## Description
Vision sensor detected retaining collar positioned 55 mm along the cylindrical body instead of flush seated against the left journal shoulder datum.

## Confirmed Root Cause
Assembly press axial stop cylinder linear scale feedback cable had loose connector, causing press ram to halt prematurely 55 mm short of mechanical positive stop.

## Resolution & Corrective Action
Replaced linear encoder connector with IP67 sealed M12 connector, calibrated press stop distance with dial gauge, and added optical proximity sensor verifying collar seating.

## Containment Action
Disassembled 18 incorrectly positioned collars using hydraulic puller; inspected shaft journal for galling.
