# Nonconformance Report: NCR-141
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Decoy: Surface scratches on metal nut from conveyor chute vibration friction
- **Date:** 2026-08-14
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** CNC-03
- **Defect Classification:** surface_scratch
- **Defect Family:** surface_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Superficial scratches observed on outer hexagonal flats of metal nuts following gravity feed transition. Symptoms mimic unloader gripper abrasion.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Decoy investigation: Grippers inspected and verified clean. Root cause confirmed as excessive slope and lack of UHMW wear lining on the inter-stage gravity chute causing parts to tumble against sheet metal welds.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Installed smooth UHMW polyethylene chute liners and reduced slide inclination from 35 degrees to 18 degrees to eliminate tumbling impact.
- **Containment Action:** 100% visual screening; polish rework acceptable for non-critical non-flange surfaces.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
