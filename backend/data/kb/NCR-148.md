# Nonconformance Report: NCR-148
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Thread damage: Flattened thread crests and torn lead on precision fastener screws
- **Date:** 2026-09-15
- **Part Number:** SCR-HEX-M8X40
- **Proxy Category:** screw
- **Machine:** THREAD-ROLLER-01
- **Defect Classification:** thread_damage
- **Defect Family:** machining_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection identified flattened thread ridges and micro-tears along top 4 threads of M8 fastener screws. Pitch gauge indicated crest truncation.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Thread rolling die reached 82,000 cycles without scheduled regrind (limit: 75,000 cycles). Die profile wear caused insufficient penetration into blank root diameter.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced cylindrical rolling dies with matched Kennametal carbide set, re-calibrated center distance gauge, and set PLC cycle counter alarm to 70,000 cycles.
- **Containment Action:** Scrapped 85 non-repairable threaded screws; quarantined lot for 100% Go/No-Go ring gauge testing.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
