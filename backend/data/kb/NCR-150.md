# Nonconformance Report: NCR-150
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Missing component: Cut and missing gate lead pin on power transistor package
- **Date:** 2026-09-24
- **Part Number:** TR-TO220-40A
- **Proxy Category:** transistor
- **Machine:** LEAD-TRIM-01
- **Defect Classification:** missing_component
- **Defect Family:** component_integrity
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection flagged missing gate pin (pin 1) on TO-220 transistor package. Anomaly score 0.94; lead completely absent from terminal body.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Shear blade edge chipped on lead forming die station 1, causing excessive tensile fracture that tore the lead flush with the molded epoxy casing.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced shear blade punch assembly, reduced shear clearance to 0.02 mm, and added acoustic emission impact sensor to detect tooling notch chips.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantined 450 units from reel #R-8821; automated vision reel sorter rejected 12 defective components.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
