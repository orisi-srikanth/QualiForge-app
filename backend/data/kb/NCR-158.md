# Nonconformance Report: NCR-158
- **Title:** Longitudinal surface scratches on precision shaft journal from swarf chips in coolant nozzle
- **Defect Type:** surface_scratch
- **Defect Family:** surface_defect
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-04
- **Date:** 2026-10-10
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative

## Description
Visual inspection flagged longitudinal linear scratches along finish turned journal face. Average scratch depth 0.012 mm exceeding Ra 0.8 um surface finish specification.

## Confirmed Root Cause
Coolant filtration bag tore, allowing recirculating steel chips to lodge between wiping pad and rotating shaft workpiece during finish turning pass.

## Resolution & Corrective Action
Installed 10-micron magnetic chip separator upstream of coolant tank, replaced torn filter bag, and upgraded wiper pads to wear-resistant polyurethane.

## Containment Action
Quarantine 60 units from lot; polish rework qualified on centerless polishing lathe.
