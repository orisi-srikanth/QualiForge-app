# Nonconformance Report: NCR-143
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Decoy: Dent deformation on transistor heat sink case from pneumatic ejector over-pressure
- **Date:** 2026-08-22
- **Part Number:** TR-TO220-40A
- **Proxy Category:** transistor
- **Machine:** FORMING-PRESS-02
- **Defect Classification:** dent_deformation
- **Defect Family:** geometric_deformation
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Localized impact indentation observed on the copper mounting flange of TO-220 power transistor packages. Symptoms resemble hopper drop damage.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Decoy investigation: Bulk feeding chutes are foam-padded. Root cause confirmed as pneumatic parts-ejector cylinder air regulator failure, operating at 7.2 bar instead of specified 2.5 bar, driving parts against the metal catch bin.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced failed pressure regulator with lockable precision regulator set to 2.2 bar and added low-bounce silicone catch tray.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantined 250 transistor units; 100% co-planarity gauge check of mounting tabs.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
