# Nonconformance Report: NCR-024
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Bore dimension undersized due to thermal contraction
- **Date:** 2026-04-02
- **Part Number:** BH-2026-202
- **Machine:** CNC-01
- **Defect Classification:** Undersized bore diameter
- **Incident Description:** Bearing housing bore measured immediately after roughing cut while part temperature was 48C. Part shrank 0.04 mm upon cooling to room temperature.

- **Corrective Resolution:** Enforced 15-minute thermal stabilization soaking station before final QC gauge pass.
