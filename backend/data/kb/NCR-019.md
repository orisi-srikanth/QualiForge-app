# Nonconformance Report: NCR-019
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Dimensional increase associated with confirmed tool wear
- **Date:** 2026-03-14
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-02
- **Defect Classification:** Oversized shaft diameter
- **Incident Description:** Shaft outer diameter drifted positive toward and past USL (50.05 mm) during latter half of high-volume night shift batch. Machine telemetry showed tool cycle counter exceeded recommended threshold of 600 cycles, reaching 1,180 cycles before detection.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Progressive tool flank wear (VB > 0.35 mm) on carbide finish turning insert due to tool usage exceeding the scheduled preventive replacement threshold.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced indexable carbide insert with coated grade Kennametal CNMG432-RP. Recalibrated tool setter offset register Z and X. Updated SOP-044 to mandate tool change alarm at 650 cycles.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
