# Nonconformance Report: NCR-055
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Tapered shaft diameter due to headstock misalignment
- **Date:** 2026-07-04
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-04
- **Defect Classification:** Tapered cylinder geometry
- **Incident Description:** Front journal measured 50.01 mm but rear journal measured 50.07 mm across 180 mm span.
