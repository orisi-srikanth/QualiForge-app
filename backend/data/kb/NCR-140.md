# Nonconformance Report: NCR-140
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Surface scratch defects on metal nut flange caused by abrasive unloader gripper wear
- **Date:** 2026-08-10
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** CNC-02
- **Defect Classification:** surface_scratch
- **Defect Family:** surface_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection flagged circumferential and longitudinal linear scratches along the bearing face of hexagonal flange metal nuts. Anomaly score 0.88, localized ROI on upper flange rim.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Hard carbide swarf embedded in the polyurethane lining of the automated unloader robot gripper fingers, creating repetitive scoring during part extraction.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced polyurethane gripper pads with hardened brass jaws, installed automated high-pressure air blow-off nozzle to clear swarf prior to grip, and updated daily maintenance checklist.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantine 180 units from Lot L-2026-941; optical sorting station verified conforming units.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
