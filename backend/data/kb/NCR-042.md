# Nonconformance Report: NCR-042
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Oversized shaft caused by incorrect tool offset register input
- **Date:** 2026-06-11
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-03
- **Defect Classification:** Oversized shaft diameter
- **Incident Description:** First 10 parts of day shift produced at 50.08 mm consistently from part #1. No gradual drift; step-shift immediately following setup change.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Operator manually typed +0.035 mm instead of -0.035 mm into geometry wear offset register during morning changeover.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Mandated barcode scanning for tool offset presets; locked manual CNC register editing.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
