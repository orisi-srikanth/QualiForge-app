# Nonconformance Report: NCR-144
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Incorrect assembly: Inverted orientation of flange nuts in automated tapping fixture
- **Date:** 2026-08-28
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** TAP-AUTO-01
- **Defect Classification:** incorrect_assembly
- **Defect Family:** assembly_error
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Vision sensor detected inverted part orientation (flange facing downward rather than upward) entering the thread tapping spindle nest.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Optical orientation sensor reflector obscured by coolant mist deposits, failing to trigger the pneumatic rejection gate for upside-down parts.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Installed positive air-purge curtain over orientation sensor optic lens and added secondary mechanical mechanical poke-yoke orientation keyway.
- **Containment Action:** Machine immediately interlocked; automated station purged of 45 parts in buffer.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
