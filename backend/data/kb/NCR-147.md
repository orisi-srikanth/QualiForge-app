# Nonconformance Report: NCR-147
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Decoy: Surface contamination discoloration from oxidized rust preventive oil bath
- **Date:** 2026-09-11
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** DIP-TANK-02
- **Defect Classification:** surface_contamination
- **Defect Family:** surface_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Mottled yellowish discoloration on parts exiting preservation line. Visual appearance resembles alkaline rinse burn.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Decoy investigation: Washer nozzles confirmed clear and rinse pH normal (7.1). Root cause identified as rust preventive oil tank temperature controller exceeding 65C (setpoint: 40C), causing thermal oil oxidation.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Replaced faulty RTD temperature thermocouple, flushed oil tank, and introduced dual-redundant thermal cut-off relay.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantine 150 packaged boxes; degrease and re-apply fresh corrosion inhibitor.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
