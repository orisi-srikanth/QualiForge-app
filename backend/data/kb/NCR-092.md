# Nonconformance Report: NCR-092
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Coolant concentration breakdown leading to rapid insert crater wear
- **Date:** 2026-09-02
- **Part Number:** PS-2026-101
- **Machine:** CNC-04
- **Defect Classification:** Tool degradation / dimensional drift
- **Incident Description:** Tool insert failed after only 220 cycles with severe crater wear and burning.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Water-soluble coolant concentration dropped to 2.1% (nominal 8.0%), causing catastrophic lubricity loss.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Installed automated refractometer coolant dosing unit on CNC-04 sump.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
