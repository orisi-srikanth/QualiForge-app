# Nonconformance Report: NCR-146
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Surface contamination: Dark alkaline wash residue and blotches on metal nut finish
- **Date:** 2026-09-06
- **Part Number:** NUT-HEX-M12
- **Proxy Category:** metal_nut
- **Machine:** WASH-LINE-01
- **Defect Classification:** surface_contamination
- **Defect Family:** surface_defect
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection detected irregular brown and amber surface discoloration staining across bearing faces of machined metal nuts.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Part-washing station rinse nozzle clogged with calcium scale, leaving concentrated alkaline cleaning detergent residue that baked onto parts during hot-air drying.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Descale wash and rinse manifolds, replace spray nozzles with quick-release stainless tips, and install automated pH rinse water conductivity monitor.
- **Containment Action:** Reworked 410 stained parts through ultra-pure ultrasonic DI water bath with zero dimensional change.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
