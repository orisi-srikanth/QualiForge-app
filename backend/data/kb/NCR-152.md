# Nonconformance Report: NCR-152
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Title:** Insulation damage: Punctured PVC outer insulation jacket on wiring harness
- **Date:** 2026-10-02
- **Part Number:** CAB-IND-HARN-04
- **Proxy Category:** cable
- **Machine:** STRIP-CRIMP-01
- **Defect Classification:** insulation_or_wire_damage
- **Defect Family:** insulation_damage
- **Data Provenance:** synthetic_illustrative
- **Incident Description:** Visual inspection identified puncture marks and jacket tears exposing internal copper braiding on multi-conductor harness. Anomaly score 0.91.
- **Confirmed Root Cause:** Automatic stripping blade depth micrometer zero-shift error (+0.35 mm depth excess) penetrating both outer sheath and primary conductor dielectric.
- **Corrective Resolution:** Calibrated stripping machine servo depth stop, implemented optical laser micrometer verification of stripped length and cut depth.
- **Containment Action:** Quarantine 120 assemblies; dielectric withstand test (Hi-Pot 1500V AC) applied to adjacent lots.
- **Epistemic Classification:** CONFIRMED_ROOT_CAUSE (Approved by Lead Quality Engineer)
